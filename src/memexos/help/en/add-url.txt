memex add-url <link> [--title <title>] [--via <channel>]

  Puts the link into raw/ according to its kind: videos are transcribed; tweets keep the author's thread and any long-form
  article; podcast episodes and audio links are transcribed locally; other pages keep their main text (PDFs are converted).
  Values that look like secrets are replaced before writing. Compiling and review happen automatically afterwards.
