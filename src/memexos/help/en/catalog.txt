memex catalog stats
memex catalog list [--domain <prefix>] [--type <content type>] [--evidence <evidence>] [--usage <usage>] [-q <words>]
memex catalog drafts      provisional categories that have not been confirmed yet

  Every source has a classification (standard: .memex/taxonomy.md in the knowledge base):
    domain: Top/Sub. "Top (provisional: name)" is a category not confirmed yet: read it as the top-level domain
    content type, level, evidence (first-hand test, official or original author, academic research, third-party report,
    personal opinion, promotional), usage, durability, and a one-line scope note

  Examples
    memex catalog list --domain AI --evidence "first-hand test"
    memex catalog list --type showcase -q 3D
