memex get <page name or path> [--from <line>] [--lines <count>]

  Any of these work: a page name without folder or .md (memex get "Reciprocal rank fusion"), a [[link]] copied from a page,
  a path (memex get "wiki/topics/Search.md"). Names are matched without regard to case. Read long pages in parts with
  --from / --lines. When nothing matches, close page names are suggested; otherwise use memex search.
