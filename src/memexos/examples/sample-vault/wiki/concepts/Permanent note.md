# Permanent note

A permanent note is a short note that states one idea in your own words, stands on its own without the original
context, and links to related notes; it is the unit a [[Zettelkasten]] is built from ([[Luhmann's slip-box, explained]]).

- In the method popularised by Sönke Ahrens, quick notes taken while reading are turned into permanent notes, which
  are then linked to existing ones.
- The owner adopted the practice: one or two permanent notes after each article, with links and without copied quotes
  ([[Setting up a reading and review routine]]).
