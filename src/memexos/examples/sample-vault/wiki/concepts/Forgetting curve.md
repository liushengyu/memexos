# Forgetting curve

The forgetting curve describes how retention of newly learned material declines over time: steeply in the first hours
and the first day, then more slowly ([[How spaced repetition works]]).

- It goes back to [[Hermann Ebbinghaus]]'s experiments published in 1885, in which he memorised nonsense syllables and
  measured how much effort relearning a list took after a delay.
- The same experiments showed **savings** (relearning is faster than learning the first time) and the **spacing
  effect** (spread-out reviews hold better than massed ones), the basis of [[Spaced repetition]].
