# Leitner system

The Leitner system is a paper-based form of [[Spaced repetition]] with flashcards sorted into a few boxes, described by
the German science journalist Sebastian Leitner in 1972 ([[How spaced repetition works]]).

- Every card starts in the first box.
- A correct answer moves the card up one box.
- A wrong answer sends the card back to the first box.
- Lower boxes are reviewed often (the first box every day), higher boxes less and less often, so known cards drift
  towards rarely reviewed boxes while difficult cards keep coming back.
