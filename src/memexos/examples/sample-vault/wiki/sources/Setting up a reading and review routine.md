# Setting up a reading and review routine

A session snapshot in which the owner decided how to keep what they read.

## Key points

- **Background.** Paper [[Leitner system]] boxes were tried in December and abandoned after two weeks: carrying the
  boxes did not fit the day and the daily first box kept being skipped.
- **Decisions.**
  1. After reading, write one or two [[Permanent note]]s in their own words, linked to related notes; no copied quotes.
  2. Flashcards only for what must be recalled without looking it up: at most three per article, one fact per card.
  3. [[Anki]] with FSRS: digital so it goes everywhere, and FSRS adapts to the owner's own review history.
  4. Fifteen minutes every morning before email; a skipped day is not doubled up.
- **Open question.** Whether fifteen minutes stays enough once the deck passes a few hundred cards; to be checked after a
  month by the average daily review count and the share of failed reviews.
