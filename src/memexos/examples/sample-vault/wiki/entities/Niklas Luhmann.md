# Niklas Luhmann

German sociologist (1927–1998), known for a theory of society as a system of communications and for his
[[Zettelkasten]] ([[Luhmann's slip-box, explained]]).

- Published dozens of books and hundreds of articles and credited much of it to his slip-box.
- Filled it over more than four decades with about 90,000 index cards.
- Described the box as a partner in conversation in the 1981 essay *Kommunikation mit Zettelkästen*.
- The box is now at Bielefeld University, whose Niklas Luhmann archive is digitising it and publishing it online.
