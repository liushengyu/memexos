# Hermann Ebbinghaus

German psychologist who published *Über das Gedächtnis* (Memory) in 1885, the first systematic experiments on
forgetting ([[How spaced repetition works]]).

- Served as his own subject, memorising lists of nonsense syllables so that meaning would not help.
- Measured the effort needed to relearn a list after a delay; his results describe the [[Forgetting curve]].
- Also found savings (relearning is faster) and the spacing effect behind [[Spaced repetition]].
