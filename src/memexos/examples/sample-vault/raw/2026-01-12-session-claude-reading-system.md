# Setting up a reading and review routine (session snapshot)

What was decided in a working session about how to keep what I read.

## Background

I read a lot of technical articles and forget most of them within weeks. I tried paper Leitner boxes in December and
gave up after two weeks: carrying the boxes around did not fit my day, and I kept skipping the daily first box.

## Decisions

1. **Read, then write a permanent note.** After an article I write one or two notes in my own words in the knowledge
   base, each with links to related notes. No copied quotes.
2. **Cards only for what must be recalled without looking it up.** For each article at most three flashcards, one fact
   per card.
3. **Anki with FSRS.** Digital, so it goes everywhere; FSRS because it adapts to my own review history.
4. **Fifteen minutes every morning**, before email. If I skip a day, I do not double up the next day.

## Open question

Whether fifteen minutes is enough once the deck grows past a few hundred cards. To check after a month: average daily
review count and how many reviews I fail.
