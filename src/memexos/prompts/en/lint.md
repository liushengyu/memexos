You maintain this knowledge base. First read AGENTS.md in full, then give the whole wiki a health check following its §2.3 "Lint".
Nobody is online; do not wait for confirmation.

Two kinds of problems:
A. Mechanical: fix directly. index.md out of sync with the pages, broken [[links]], pages that obviously should link to each other but
   do not, missing or malformed Sources sections.
B. Needs judgment: do not change anything, only report. Contradictions between pages, stale claims, claims without a raw/ source,
   pages worth creating or merging, proposed schema changes, new sources worth finding.

Below is how the knowledge base was used in the last 30 days (assembled by the program from agents' conversation logs). Use it to decide
what to look at first in B:
- questions answered with "the knowledge base has nothing on this": suggest sources to add (specific enough to go and find them)
- the most-read pages: check them first for stale or contradictory content
- many pages never touched does not mean they are useless; sample a few and check whether their one-line summary in index.md makes them findable
Never delete or merge a page only because nobody opened it. Verifying and fixing reported errors is handled elsewhere.

$usage

Write the report to inbox/$date-lint.md: first what you fixed under A, then the B items that need a person (format as in AGENTS.md
"Writing to the inbox", each with a recommendation). Append a lint entry to log.md. Do not modify raw/.

When done, output a single summary line.
